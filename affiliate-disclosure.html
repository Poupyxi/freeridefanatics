Affiliate disclosure

Some product links may generate a commission, but affiliate availability does not determine which riders, components or results appear in the database.

How affiliate links work

Some external product links are affiliate links. If a visitor follows one of these links and completes a qualifying purchase, RidersFanatics may receive a commission without increasing the visitor's purchase price.

Editorial independence

Equipment is recorded because it is publicly associated with a tracked rider, not because a retailer offers a commission. A product without an affiliate link can rank above a monetised product, and many tracked components have no commercial link at all.

Buying decisions

Race equipment may be a prototype, a team-specific tune or a configuration that differs from a retail product. Visitors should verify compatibility, sizing, specification, warranty and local availability with the manufacturer or retailer before buying.
